Our best feats

Things a phone was never supposed to do.

None of this is a remote desktop or a Linux box hiding in a container. It all runs on the phone, today.

Native Plasma

KDE Plasma, compiled for Android itself

KWin, plasmashell and the KDE apps are built with the Android NDK against Android's own libc and draw with your phone's own GPU driver. There's no proot, no chroot and no Linux system image. It's one app with a panel, windows, a file manager and the Kickoff menu, over your phone's wallpaper.

Flathub

All of Flathub, in an app store

Agora, DeskDroid's store, browses Flathub by category. Our ddmask layer runs desktop Linux apps on Android, so real Flatpak apps install and open.

GPU

Adreno acceleration

KWin runs on Adreno with GLES 3, and Linux apps' OpenGL goes to Android's GLES through virgl, the same driver the desktop uses.

x86 on ARM

Box64 and FEX-Emu built in

x86-64 programs run on your ARM phone: ddmask hands them to Box64, and x86-64 Flatpak apps run through FEX-Emu.

X11 too

Xwayland inside KWin

Older X11-only apps like VLC open as normal desktop windows next to Wayland apps, with their own taskbar icons.

Sound

PipeWire, EasyEffects, ViPER

A full PipeWire and WirePlumber sound server. Every Linux app's sound can go through EasyEffects or ViPER FX on its way to your speakers.

At your desk

Plug in a monitor. Get a PC.

Use DeskDroid on the phone screen, or connect a display and peripherals and work like you would on a laptop. Your Android apps come along too.

  • External monitors with their own scale. The desktop takes over the screen instead of Android's desktop mode.
  • Your phone becomes the touchpad, with edge swipes, a menu button and a screen that goes dark when idle.
  • Keyboard and mouse, or real touch with a corner-swipe navbar. Gboard and SwiftKey swipe typing work in desktop text fields.
  • Android apps in Kickoff, sorted by kind, and files open in Android apps through Open With.
  • Android widgets on the desktop, plus a shared clipboard and media keys.
  • Battery, network and power profiles in the tray: Power Save, Balanced and Performance.
DD Party

A desktop with a personality.

DeskDroid's own look: near-black surfaces, magenta, rainbow accents and chamfered corners, with a matching icon pack, window borders, splash screen, terminal and editor colours. It also installs on any Plasma 6 PC, with a Glass variant that blurs everything behind it.

  • A full icon pack for folders, file types, apps and devices.
  • GTK apps match through a DD Party build of Breeze GTK.
  • Etherium, our browser, wears it too.
Etherium

A full desktop browser, on your terms.

Etherium is our own build of Firefox 160 for DeskDroid. No Mozilla account, no Sync, less data collection, and sound that plays through the desktop's sound server so your effects hear it.
